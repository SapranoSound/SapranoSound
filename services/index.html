Services

Vocal recording, music production, and video editing.

Three services, one engineer. Sessions run at Checkmark Audio Studio B in Albuquerque — or remotely and on location.

01 — Vocal recording

Getting the take, then keeping the best of it.

For singers, rappers, spoken word, and voice work that needs clean vocals without a complicated session.

Who it’s for

Artists recording in Albuquerque who want a treated room, a second set of ears, and files ready for the next step.

How it runs

  • Level and headphone setup, then a warm-up pass
  • Takes recorded and reviewed together, pass by pass
  • Selections made in the room, not months later
  • Files organised before you leave

What to bring

  • A rough guide, reference track, or demo
  • Final lyrics, and the tempo you’re working at
  • Your timeline and deadline

02 — Music production

From a rough idea to a track that holds together.

For artists with songs in progress who need arrangement, sound direction, and a finished record.

Who it’s for

Artists and producers with demos or half-finished sessions who want the track built with intent, not extra layers for their own sake.

How it runs

  • References and direction agreed before work starts
  • Arrangement and sound selection shaped around the song
  • Sessions in the studio or file-based
  • Revisions scoped up front

What to bring

  • Demos in whatever state they’re in
  • Reference tracks that describe the target
  • Lyrics, credits, and who else is involved

03 — Video editing

Picture that matches the audio.

Performance, promo, and behind-the-scenes footage — cut to work with the music, not against it.

Who it’s for

Artists and small teams with footage who need a finished cut, not a hard drive of clips.

How it runs

  • Footage reviewed and selects agreed first
  • Cut paced to the audio
  • Rough cut, revision pass, then final delivery
  • Exported for the platforms you release on

What to bring

  • Footage files or a working link
  • The audio or master it should cut to
  • Brand assets, titles, and your deadline

How sessions work

In the room, remote, or on location.

Recording happens at Checkmark Audio Studio B, 5413 Lomas Blvd NE, Albuquerque — a working control room.

Production and editing can run remotely: files exchanged, versions reviewed. Travel is available when a project needs Tony elsewhere.

Every project starts the same way: a message with what you’re making, when it’s due, and where you are.
